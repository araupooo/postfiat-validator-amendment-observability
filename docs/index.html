Measured over 198 consecutive validated ledgers (seq 8049400–8049599, 198 fetched of 200 requested, total burn 772 drops). The previously cited rounded figure of 10 drops/ledger was the per-transaction base fee, not a per-ledger measurement — the correction factor is 2.56x. Per-ledger burn = sum of the Fee field of that ledger's transactions; fees are destroyed (ApplyStateTable::destroyXRP), not paid to anyone. Cross-check: total_coins delta between consecutive ledgers equals the burn in 195/197 pairs; the 2 mismatches are fully explained by the 2 unfetched ledgers (implied burns 30 and 20 drops).

Limitations

  • The fee-burn figure covers a 7-minute window; activity varies by time of day.
  • Per-validator participation and amendment-majority trends need the validations stream / repeated snapshots; not measured here.
  • The 12-drop transaction and the 500x median fee are reported as observed; their causes were not established.
  • Only JSON-RPC over HTTPS was probed; WebSocket APIs were not used.
